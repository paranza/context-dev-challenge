# Challenge brief: The Web Is Your Real-Time Database

**Organizer:** Context.dev  
**Event:** Junction 2026: European Tech Renaissance  
**Submission status in the supplied brief:** Open  
**Participant context:** Solo builder; this is a secondary main challenge, separate from the participant's primary challenge.

## Challenge goal

Build a product that reacts to the live web. Turn information scattered across websites into decisions, actions, or experiences that are difficult to create with stale or manually collected data.

## Context.dev

Context.dev describes its product as a web-data layer for AI. Its APIs turn websites, brands, companies, people, and news into structured context for software and agents. The supplied brief says teams including Supermemory, Mintlify, and Upstash use it, and describes the company as having gone through YC S26 and raised a $9M seed led by General Catalyst.

## Inspiration in the brief

- Compare live GPU rental prices across cloud providers.
- Rank founders and startups by activity on X.
- Monitor newly disclosed security vulnerabilities.
- Personalize onboarding using company data and brand assets.

Suggested themes include live indicators from product prices or job listings, agents triggered by website or news changes, onboarding personalized from a signup email, and fast fact-checking with sources. Other creative ideas are welcome.

## Judging criteria

Each category is weighted equally at 20%:

1. **Real-world impact:** Does the product address a real business or consumer need?
2. **Creative use of live web data:** Does it combine sources or use the data in an interesting way?
3. **Completeness of demo:** How ready is it for production?
4. **Technical execution:** How good are data latency and accuracy?
5. **Real-timeness:** Does it react to change over time, or only take a snapshot?
