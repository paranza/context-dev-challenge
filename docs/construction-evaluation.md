# Construction research evaluation

Checked **15 November 2026**. Fifteen real contractor websites were run through the application; **twelve** are approved for the company picker. Sol agents reviewed geography, actual trade/lot, scale, deadlines and official evidence, then reran cases after fixes. Offline regression tests are separate from these live requests.

A pass means at least one useful, current construction lead with independently checked official sources. It does not establish formal bidder eligibility or guarantee every result is equally useful. Unknown qualifications remain Needs review. The fictional sample workspace demonstrates all three statuses without manufacturing live findings.

## Corrections verified

- Vitali changed from foreign-only output to four Italian leads, three in Lombardia.
- Decker retained regional structural-shell work rather than broad civil engineering.
- BESIX recovered Belgian headquarters and Belgian opportunities; an operating market is no longer assumed to be home.
- NCC recovered from a false construction-profile rejection and returned five relevant Swedish leads.
- YIT and Collini no longer display superseded notices as separate opportunities. Stable procedure IDs preserve refresh comparisons.
- STRABAG specialist electrical, HVAC, window and carpentry lots no longer qualify from a broad school-building CPV.
- Bottoli building/restoration wording now selects its actual core trade.
- Unresolved qualification or capacity criteria cannot produce Likely fit merely because the model marked them non-mandatory.

## Cases withheld

- **Collini:** final scan missed published hospice experience. Earlier sewer results had a confirmed OG6 standalone qualification gap. It is not a vetted picker example.
- **DAREMA:** returned budgets above €5.4m did not establish a useful scale-appropriate lead for the small contractor. Independent below-threshold opportunities were found outside the app; discovery needs more work.
- **NGE:** latest result has unresolved relaunch status, all-lots obligations and an urgent deadline. It is excluded from the picker.

## Limits

Only initial live result pages were independently reviewed. Complete procurement documents and latest national amendments still need checking. National search is an indexed subset. These tests establish useful demo leads; they are not an exhaustive procurement export or an eligibility decision.

[Structured evaluation and source links](construction-evaluation.json) retain the approved and excluded cases. Raw provider responses, signed continuation tokens and local credentials are not committed.
