Tender Fit RadarBest Use of Context.devTechnical execution · 20% / 30%08

No source, no claim.

01

Verified only if the page was read

A buyer, deadline or value shows as verified only when its cited page loaded successfully.

02

Official sources are the only proof

The notice and its linked documents. Third-party pages are context, never evidence.

03

Silence is not a mismatch

Missing evidence becomes “Needs review”, never a rejection. Fit is advice, not a legal eligibility verdict.

04

Failures are shown, not hidden

Every portal reports searched, failed or not searchable. Unread documents are counted.

Latency
  • Supplier reads in parallel 5 at once
  • Portal searches in flight 6 at a time
  • Results reach the screen per notice
  • Paging with signed cursor 6 / page
Quality
  • Automated tests passing 58 / 58
  • Typecheck + production build clean
  • API key leaves the server never
