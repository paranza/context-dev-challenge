# Tender Fit Radar

## Register

brand

## Users

Construction suppliers deciding which public tenders across the EU and EEA deserve their time. For the Junction demo, a first-time visitor must understand the idea and inspect a complete evidence workflow quickly.

## Product Purpose

Turn a supplier website into researched public procurement opportunities. Search official sources in 30 EU/EEA countries and research notices in pages of up to 20. Connect each tender requirement to supplier evidence, preserve source links and uncertainty, explain incomplete research and country coverage, and compare fresh scans with the previous scan stored in the browser.

## Brand Personality

Clear, considered, capable. Combine Context.dev's precise product presentation, Corgi's confident visual personality, and BoardUI's clean agentic components through an original orange identity. The entry experience uses expressive imagery and explanatory motion; the analysis workspace keeps the focus on requirements, evidence, and sources.

## Anti-references

No green or purple branding. Avoid generic AI gradients, decorative dashboards, fabricated fit percentages, unsupported monitoring features, and animations that imply backend events the server does not provide.

## Design Principles

- Put the supplier website and the next useful action first.
- Give evidence, uncertainty, and original sources equal visibility.
- Make the first scan approachable and the research workspace efficient.
- Separate illustrative demo data from fresh research and saved scans.
- Use motion to acknowledge actions and real state changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, responsive single-column layouts, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone for evidence status.
